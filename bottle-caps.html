Bottle Caps

02/09/2026 10:00:00 to 12:00:00 TCT

  1. Enter your API key and press Count.
  2. The page reads your crime log for the comp window and adds up Bottle Caps gained from searching the beach.
  3. Press Copy and paste the result in Discord.

Full Access key required. Create one at Preferences, API Keys.

How your key is used

The key is sent from your browser to api.torn.com only. It is not stored, logged, or sent anywhere else. Reloading the page clears it.

The log endpoint does not work with Limited or Minimal keys. You can delete the key after the comp.
